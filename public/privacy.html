Privacy policy

Ranking Reports · Last updated 27 November 2026

Ranking Reports is the internal dashboard Rooster Partners' SEO team uses to track how its own websites rank on Google. It is not open to the public: only staff whose access has been approved can sign in.

What it collects

  • Staff sign-in. The email address you sign in with, and whether your access is approved. This is used only to decide who can see the dashboard.
  • Google Search Console, read-only. When an administrator connects a Google account, the dashboard asks Google for read-only Search Console access (webmasters.readonly) and that account's email address. It reads search performance for the team's own websites: the searches they appeared for, by country, with clicks, impressions and average position. It cannot change anything in Search Console.

How it is stored

  • The Google access token is stored encrypted on the server and is never sent to anyone's browser.
  • Search Console results are kept for up to 6 hours, so that every page view doesn't ask Google again.
  • The connected account's email address is shown to administrators, so they can see which account is connected.

How it is used

Google data is shown only inside the dashboard, to approved staff. It is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Ranking Reports' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

  • An administrator can click Disconnect at any time. That revokes the access at Google and deletes the stored token and every saved result.
  • The Google account's owner can also remove the access from their Google account, at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions

Ask the Ranking Reports administrators.
